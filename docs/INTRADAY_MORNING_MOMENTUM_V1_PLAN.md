# Morning momentum v1: frozen research plan

This is a new intraday hypothesis, not a change to the live daily pullback strategy. A completed 11:00 a.m. Eastern five-minute bar determines each day's signal. For the same 20 liquid U.S. stocks and ETFs used by the prior intraday research, measure each symbol's return from its 9:30 a.m. bar open to its 11:00 a.m. bar close. Require both bars, a price of at least $5, and a morning gain of at least 0.6%. Rank eligible symbols by that return and select at most two. Enter at each selected symbol's next five-minute bar open, allocating 40% of then-current account equity per position with no leverage. A 1% stop, including adverse opening gaps, or a 3:50 p.m. market exit ends each position. Simulate the scheduled exit at the 3:50 p.m. bar open so the rule does not use that bar's closing price. Charge 0.1% of position notional round trip. The daily decision cannot be repeated and no position carries overnight.

Do not optimize these numbers after viewing the result. Evaluate July–December 2025 as a development diagnostic and January–June 2026 as the fixed confirmation window on every paginated Alpaca IEX five-minute bar. The confirmation must contain at least 50 completed trades, positive compounded account return after cost, and no worse than 15% maximum drawdown; also report a 0.2% cost stress, each quarter, gaps, and missing-bar exclusions. The already inspected periods make this a challenging historical check, not a pristine untouched holdout. If it passes, run the frozen version on July–September 2026 without changing parameters. It still needs an exact-version paper worker, broker stop and exit lifecycle, and a separate live worker gate before it can receive live capital.

The hypothesis is that an unusually strong morning move may persist into the afternoon. The [intraday return-pattern research](https://arxiv.org/abs/1005.3535) motivates a test of intraday predictability but does not establish that this particular rule will profit after costs.
