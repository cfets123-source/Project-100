# Monthly whole-share ETF time-series momentum v1: frozen plan

This distinct hypothesis follows the published 12-month time-series momentum finding ([Moskowitz, Ooi, and Pedersen, 2012](https://fairmodel.econ.yale.edu/ec439/mosk.pdf)) but implements a long-only, cash-constrained ETF version. The fixed 14-ETF U.S.-listed universe is in the evaluator. Use raw IEX daily bars so historical whole-share affordability uses the price payable then. At each month-end close, rank ETFs by positive 252-session price return, requiring a close above the 200-session simple moving average. Hold cash if none qualifies. Exit any old position at the first session of the next month and buy the strongest affordable whole-share ETF at the second session open, allowing one session for cash settlement. Allocate no more than 95% of cash. A 10% GTC-style stop protects the full-share position until monthly exit. Model gap-through-stop at the worse open and 0.1% round-trip cost, with 0.2% stress. No leverage, shorting, or fractional overnight orders.

Freeze the rule before retrieving pre-2021 raw data or reading this rule's return. Evaluate 2017–2021, 2022–2024, and 2025–September 2026 separately with $100 starting cash in each period and prior bars only for indicator warmup. The 2025–2026 years were used by other hypotheses and are not a pristine untouched holdout. An execution-path screen requires positive modeled return in all three periods at 0.1% cost, positive recent return at 0.2% cost, maximum drawdown better than -25% in each period, and at least 10 completed trades in the recent period. This screen is intentionally hard; even a pass only permits exact-version paper implementation and forward broker evidence, not immediate live capital. Report unavailable whole-share entries, open positions at period end, IEX data limits, and corporate-action/dividend limits.
