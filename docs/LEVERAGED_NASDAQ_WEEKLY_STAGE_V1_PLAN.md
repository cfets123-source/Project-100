# Leveraged Nasdaq weekly rotation: milestone test plan

Frozen 2026-09-23 before reading TQQQ or SQQQ returns from the issuer file. This is a distinct payoff route from unlevered spot crypto: hold one 3x daily Nasdaq-100 ETF, its inverse counterpart, or cash. The instruments are TQQQ and SQQQ. The source is ProShares' historical daily NAV CSV; NAV is a research proxy, not an executable exchange fill. This screen does not use an options or margin account.

Use the first trading day in each ISO calendar week as the decision day. At that day's close, rank TQQQ and SQQQ by their **prior trading day's** 20-session NAV return. Hold 100% of the higher positive-return fund from decision-day NAV close to the next close; otherwise hold cash. Ties break alphabetically. Stay in that position until the next weekly decision. Deduct 0.25% of account equity for each entry and exit leg at the decision-day change. No additional deposits, explicit margin, intraday stops, or post-result parameter changes. The proxy assumes fractional ETF positions are possible; broker eligibility must be verified before execution.

Evaluate 2024-09-23–2025-09-19 as an earlier comparison and 2025-09-22–2026-09-21 as the later period, using the preceding 20 sessions only for warmup. Record net daily account returns including cash days, first $500/$1,000 touch, ending equity, drawdown, turnover, and zero-cost sensitivity. For the later period, compute descriptive five-day block resamples using the available trading sessions. Any missing dates, splits, or inconsistent NAV/prior-NAV relationships must be investigated before accepting results. Reaching a milestone in one historical window would be a research lead, not proof of a repeatable live strategy.
