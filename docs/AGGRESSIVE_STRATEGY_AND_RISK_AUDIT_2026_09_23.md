# Aggressive strategy and app-risk audit — 23 September 2026

The user wants a high-upside, high-volatility route through $100 → $500 → $1,000 and later milestones. The current live equity worker is materially more conservative than that brief. This is a factual code audit, not a request to change a live order or reset an active halt.

| Where | Current behavior | Effect on the user's objective |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `backend/app/core/config.py` | Defaults to 1% account risk per trade, 3% daily-loss cutoff, 6% weekly and 12% total-drawdown cutoffs, two allowed positions, and 50% maximum equity in one position. Margin, options, shorts, and leverage are disabled. | Even a strong signal has limited dollar upside from a $100 account. These are real sizing constraints, not just dashboard labels. |
| `backend/app/runtime/alpaca_live_worker.py` | Returns without seeking a new entry whenever *any* broker position exists; scans five default symbols and asks for one signal. | Effective live concurrency is one position despite `MAX_POSITIONS=2`; the strategy also cannot exploit several independent signals per cycle. |
| `backend/app/strategies/daily_trend_pullback.py` | One-position daily 50-day-trend/1.5%-pullback selector with 3% stop and 6% target. | This rule, frequency, and reward range were never designed to reach the first 5× waypoint quickly. |
| `backend/app/services/capital_stages.py` | Paper-stage scoreboard defaults to $1,000, requires 50 closed trades, a 10% maximum drawdown, and other quality metrics. It now supports an **explicit optional $500 waypoint**. | The $500 checkpoint can be used for a new paper-stage record; existing persisted state remains on its $1,000 policy until deliberately migrated. This is a metric gate, not a live position-size control. |

The low risk caps are **not the only bottleneck**. The existing exact-rule two-position equity/ETF exploratory test increased nominal per-entry risk from 0.5% to 2% and still lost money in all three scenarios ([recorded result](EXPANDED_TWO_POSITION_EXPLORATORY_RESULT.md)). The separate 2025–2026 weekly crypto test was 100% invested when signaled and lost even at zero modeled trading cost ([recorded result](CRYPTO_WEEKLY_BREAKOUT_STAGE_V3_RESULT.md)). The weekly leveraged-ETF test had exposure to a 3x daily fund, yet the timing rule lagged TQQQ buy-and-hold ([recorded result](LEVERAGED_NASDAQ_WEEKLY_STAGE_V1_RESULT.md)). Thus simply widening a cap or adding positions to these exact rules would enlarge a measured loss, not turn it into an edge.

**Build direction:** retain broker-order reconciliation, maximum-loss accounting, and protective-exit mechanics. Separate the current live worker from a new, explicitly versioned high-upside research lane. The first lane to test is small-premium, long-only options or another bounded-loss contract that can fit $100, with account risk specified as **premium dollars** and a payoff that can reach the $500 waypoint. Predeclare the signal and exit, record real bid/ask/size and all fees, test full account equity through wins and losses, then paper-test the exact code path. Its sizing can be materially larger than 1% only if the measured premium-loss distribution and actual broker permissions support it. The current E*TRADE adapter has read-only option-chain access but not a completed execution or historical quote path ([venue screen](GOAL_FIRST_VENUE_SCREEN_2026_09_23.md)).

The operating protections and the strategy's economic ambition answer different questions. A verified stop/exit and accurate ledger are needed at any risk level. The user can choose to risk more capital per opportunity; whether an opportunity has positive net expectancy still needs direct evidence. No strategy or risk setting was promoted to live by this audit.
