# Weekly global and sector ETF rotation v1: frozen research plan

This is one new long-only hypothesis based on medium-term relative momentum and trend, not a grid search. Fixed U.S.-listed ETF universe: XLF, XLE, XLP, XLU, XLRE, XBI, KRE, HYG, TLT, FXI, EWU, EEM, EWJ, VWO. FXI, EWU, EEM, EWJ, and VWO offer exposure to foreign markets through U.S.-listed ETFs; this is not direct trading on foreign exchanges. All symbols require separate broker tradability checks before paper execution.

Use raw, unadjusted Alpaca IEX daily prices from 2021 onward. On each completed Friday, rank ETFs with positive 63-session price return and a close above their 100-session simple moving average. At the next session open, buy the highest-ranked affordable whole-share ETF using no more than 90% of cash. Hold one position at a time without leverage. A 5% GTC-style stop, 10% target, or close after ten sessions ends the trade. Model adverse gaps, stop-first bars, no same-day re-entry, and 0.1% round-trip cost; stress 0.2%.

Freeze this version before reading its result. Report 2022–2024 development and 2025–September 2026 recent confirmation separately, including each year, completed trades, drawdown, unaffordable entry days, and open-position censoring. The recent window has been inspected for other stock hypotheses, so it is not pristine independent data. A research pass requires at least 20 completed recent trades, positive recent account return at both 0.1% and 0.2% cost, and maximum recent drawdown better than -20%. A pass would permit building this exact-version paper worker and collecting actual broker stop, exit, and ledger evidence; it does not authorize live capital.
