# Selected affordable momentum-20: recent confirmation

The selection was frozen in `8c26ab4` before this 2025–September 22, 2026 evaluation. Starting with $100 cash and using raw IEX daily prices for whole-share affordability, the selected `weekly-affordable-momentum-20-whole-share-v1` generated 34 completed trades at 0.1% modeled round-trip cost. Account return was **-3.97%**, and maximum drawdown was **-20.84%**. Calendar 2025 returned +1.89%; 2026 through September 22 returned -5.75%.

At the predeclared 0.2% cost stress, it generated 35 completed trades, returned **-15.20%**, and reached -22.03% drawdown. Fee-dependent cash and integer-share affordability changed the trade path, but both paths fail the positive-return and drawdown gates. The evaluator marks any open position at the sample end; it does not prove a broker exit. The model uses IEX bars, not consolidated executable quotes or actual fills.

The result does not authorize a paper execution worker or live capital for this version. Testing the other nine development rules against this same recent window now would be exploratory reuse, not an independent confirmation. Further progress needs fresh forward paper evidence or a genuinely different, separately frozen market hypothesis with execution-matched data and costs.
