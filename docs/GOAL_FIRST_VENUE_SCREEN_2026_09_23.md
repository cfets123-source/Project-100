## Strategy work that follows this screen

1. **First executable-price source:** complete E*TRADE individual production API authorization and market-data agreement. The new read-only chain endpoint can then reveal whether the account receives `REALTIME` or `DELAYED` options, and which contracts actually fit the account after fees. No credentials belong in source or chat.
2. **Candidate to freeze before testing:** event-driven *long options* on liquid US ETFs or large stocks, with entry only when a predeclared directional or volatility signal occurs. Predeclare event, timing, maximum premium, spread, liquidity, expiry, exit and failure rules. Underlying momentum alone cannot stand in for option returns because implied volatility and time decay affect the contract. The candidate is a research hypothesis, not yet a trade recommendation.
3. **Historical data needed:** timestamped option bid/ask with underlying prices and event times for development and untouched confirmation periods. A current chain, delayed quote, option last price, underlying-only backtest, or broker sandbox sample is inadequate to estimate executable return. Alpaca's free historical option feed is *indicative*, with modified quotes rather than actual OPRA quotes; Cboe sells historical NBBO interval data. Neither should be quietly represented as free executable-price history. If the history cannot be obtained within the research budget, collect forward read-only quotes first instead of fabricating a backtest.
4. **Pass condition:** account-level net compounding toward each milestone with all entry and exit fees, spread, slippage, capital lockup, assignment/expiry handling, drawdown and ruin frequency recorded. A large best-case payout or a cherry-picked winning streak is insufficient.
