# Daily liquid momentum: first-stage validation plan

This is a frozen research test for the $100 → $500 first waypoint, not a live instruction. The existing `daily-liquid-momentum-v1` signal scans SOFI, F, PLTR, TQQQ, and SQQQ. At each completed trading day's close, a candidate must close above the prior 20-session high on volume at least 1.5 times its prior 20-session average. If more than one qualifies, select the highest volume ratio, breaking ties alphabetically. Hold at most one position.

Buy at the next regular-session open only if at least one whole share is affordable with the current account equity. Allocate as many whole shares as cash allows, with no borrowing. The order must be covered by an accepted broker-side whole-share protective stop. In the historical model, exit at a 4% stop, an 8% target, or the fifth session close, whichever comes first. An opening gap through the stop fills at the open; an intraday bar touching both stop and target takes the stop first. A target exit receives the target price, even if the open gaps higher. Charge 0.2% round trip on traded notional. Idle days count in daily account returns, and the end of the sample marks any open position to market rather than pretending it exited.

Use 2024 as development and January 2025 through the most recent complete 2026 session as one confirmation interval. Report starting and ending equity, completed trades, opportunity count, blocked unaffordable entries, maximum drawdown, the first $500 touch if any, and 0.4% round-trip cost stress. If the data omit bars or volume, or if a broker GTC stop cannot be verified for the actual account and share type, those are explicit limits. Passing a code test or generating frequent signals is not a live promotion.
