# Goal-first strategy gate

Objective: grow **$100 to $1,000,000 within one year** through the sequence $100 → $1,000 → $5,000–$10,000 → $100,000 → $200,000–$300,000 → $500,000 → $1,000,000. This requires 10,000× total account growth. The gate is a screening tool, not a prediction or an automatic live-trading authorization.

With no losing trades, withdrawals, deposits, or costs, the minimum constant *net account* gain is 19.38% for each of 52 weekly trades, 3.72% for each of 252 daily trades, 1.84% for each of 504 trades, or 0.734% for each of 1,260 trades. Losses, spreads, fees, slippage, taxes, unavailable capital, and flat periods increase those requirements. A candidate that cannot plausibly produce the needed account-level compounding at the applicable stage fails before an expensive historical test.

Screen candidates in this order:

1. **Executable with the capital at that stage:** instrument, minimum order, broker permission, real quote access, trade frequency, cash settlement, and funding must work with the actual account. An available market-data feed or paper key alone does not qualify.
2. **Net edge with a defined loss:** the setup must explain why its expected payoff could exceed spread, slippage, fees, and losing trades. A large maximum payoff or a lucky backtest is not an edge. Count account-level return, not the percentage gain on a tiny option premium.
3. **Capacity across milestones:** the same rule or an explicit transition rule must remain executable when capital grows from $100 through $1 million. Show how position size, liquidity, and risk change at each threshold.
4. **Test only after those screens:** freeze the rule, use price/quote data matching the broker route, separate development from untouched confirmation, and record cost stress, drawdown, ruin frequency, and comparison with a passive benchmark. A candidate advances to paper only after it clears those tests; broker fills and protective exits then need their own lifecycle evidence.
