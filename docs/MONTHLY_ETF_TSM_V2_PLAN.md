# Monthly ETF momentum v2: frozen portfolio breaker variant

V1 had positive modeled return in 2022–2024 and 2025–September 2026, but its 2022–2024 drawdown exceeded its fixed gate. V2 preserves the exact 14 ETFs, 252-session momentum ranking, 200-session trend requirement, whole-share cash sizing, monthly rebalance and one-session settlement wait, 10% broker-style GTC stop, and 0.1%/0.2% cost cases. One portfolio rule changes: if marked account equity closes at or below 80% of its running high-water mark, issue a controlled exit at the next session open and block new entries for 63 trading sessions. The model includes the next opening gap. After cooldown, restart the high-water mark at current equity. This overlay does not prevent a gap below the 20% level. No leverage or fractional overnight order.

Freeze this code before evaluating V2. Its only historical screen is positive account return in both 2022–2024 and 2025–September 2026 at 0.1% cost, positive recent return at 0.2% cost, drawdown better than -25% and -20% respectively, and at least ten completed recent trades. These periods have been inspected for V1 and other candidates. A pass is **development evidence only**, allowing an exact-version paper worker, not live execution. The next independent evidence must be forward paper broker entry, GTC stop acceptance through sessions, controlled exit, stop cancellation, flat account, and ledger/risk reconciliation. Research orders are prohibited.
