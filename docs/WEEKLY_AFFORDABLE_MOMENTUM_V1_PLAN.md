# Weekly affordable momentum whole-share v1: frozen research plan

Use raw, unadjusted Alpaca IEX daily bars for the fixed 13-symbol universe in the evaluator. The selection emphasizes established symbols that have at times been affordable in a $100 account, but the current-symbol selection introduces survivorship and affordability bias. No shorting, leverage, options, or crypto. The full-share price at the historical entry open determines affordability; adjusted historical prices must never size a whole-share trade.

At each Friday close, rank stocks above their 100-session simple moving average and with positive 63-session return by that return. At the next session open, buy the highest-ranked affordable symbol, using at most 90% of available cash. Hold one position at a time. A 5% broker-style GTC stop, 10% target, or close after ten sessions ends the position. On a bar that touches both stop and target, apply the stop first. A gap below the stop receives the worse opening price. No re-entry on the exit day. Deduct 0.1% round-trip of traded notional and stress 0.2%.

Freeze the rules before reading the raw-price result. Treat 2022–2024 as development and 2025–September 2026 as a separate recent confirmation, while disclosing that the latter market period has already been inspected for other hypotheses. The research gate is at least 20 completed recent trades, positive compounded account return after costs, maximum drawdown no worse than 20%, and nonnegative return at 0.2% cost. Report each year, unaffordable signals, open position censoring, and evidence of corporate-action discontinuities. Passing this screen authorizes an exact-version paper execution worker and broker lifecycle collection; it does not establish profitable future trading or authorize live capital.
