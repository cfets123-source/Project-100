# Frozen split-aware monthly ETF momentum v3

V3 retains V2's 14 U.S.-listed ETFs, 252-session momentum, 200-session trend filter, one whole-share position, 95% maximum cash exposure, monthly exit/re-entry schedule, 10% broker-stop model, 20% account breaker, and 63-session cooldown. The sole research correction is corporate-action accounting: signal rankings use split-adjusted price bars, while whole-share affordability and execution use raw bars. A held position crossing a split increases share quantity and decreases the per-share entry and stop basis by the same factor. The existing 0.1% and 0.2% roundtrip cost checks and 2022–2024 / 2025–September 2026 windows remain fixed.

The December 5, 2025 2-for-1 XLE and XLU events are verified in [State Street's issuer announcement](https://investors.statestreet.com/investor-news-events/press-releases/news-details/2025/State-Street-Investment-Management-Announces-Share-Splits-for-Five-Select-Sector-SPDR-ETFs/default.aspx). The historical cache here only includes raw Alpaca IEX bars, so the analysis will derive split-adjusted bars for those issuer-confirmed events and disclose that limitation. A production paper planner must request Alpaca's `adjustment=split` bars directly and independently verify broker corporate-action handling. No results were run for V3 when this rule was frozen.
