# Bounded affordable-stock factor screen

This is a new development-only model-selection exercise, not an untouched confirmation for the already tested weekly strategy. Use the same fixed 13-symbol universe and raw IEX daily bars. Examine only 2022–2024 while choosing a rule; do not read 2025–2026 results for all alternatives.

Compare exactly ten weekly long-only rules: past-return momentum over 20, 63, or 126 sessions and short-term reversal over five or 20 sessions, each with and without a completed-close 100-session moving-average trend filter. Momentum ranks positive-return stocks from strongest to weakest; reversal ranks negative-return stocks from weakest to strongest. A candidate must be affordable at the next open as one or more whole shares within 90% of cash. All alternatives use one position, no leverage, a 5% GTC-style stop, 10% target, ten-session maximum hold, stop-first same-bar ordering, adverse gap price, no same-day re-entry, and 0.1% round-trip cost. No candidate may use test-period data to select its parameters.

Advance only if a development candidate has at least 25 completed trades, positive compounded account return over 2022–2024, and maximum drawdown better than -25%. Among qualifiers choose the highest development return divided by `1 + 3 × abs(maximum drawdown)`; ties resolve by rule name. Freeze that exact selected rule before one evaluation on 2025–September 2026. A recent result must have at least 20 completed trades, positive account return at 0.1% and 0.2% cost, and drawdown better than -20% to justify an exact-version paper worker. The recent years were already inspected for other strategies, so even a pass is not a pristine independent result and needs forward paper evidence. If no rule qualifies, none is promoted.
