# Overnight-hold test — fixed before running (2026-10-07)
Idea: buy at the close, sell at the next open ("overnight"), for SPY, QQQ, TQQQ. Compared with buy-and-hold and with
the opposite (open -> close, "intraday"). Daily Yahoo split/dividend-adjusted bars, 2011 - Sep 2026.
Costs: realistic 0.03% per side (spread + slippage on closing/opening auction orders) => 0.06% per round trip, every day.
Also shown at 0.01%/side (best case) for reference only; the decision uses 0.03%.
Periods: 2011-2020 and 2021-Sep 2026, plus last 12 months.
Adopt only if, at 0.03%/side, the overnight version (1) makes money in BOTH periods and (2) has a better
return-to-worst-drop ratio than buy-and-hold of the same ETF in BOTH periods; then it must pass the stress gate.
